FAQs

Straight Answers, No Guesswork

Answers to the questions Tecumseh homeowners ask us most.

Are your people certified and insured?

Yes to both, in writing, before we start. Ontario does not run a provincial credential for duct cleaning, so the training records, certification, and insurance a company carries are the whole story, and we are happy to show ours.

How often should a Tecumseh home be cleaned?

Every two or three years for established homes. Brand-new homes deserve one thorough cleaning right after possession to clear construction debris, then the usual cycle after that.

How will I know what was done?

Photos of each run before and after, walked through with you on site. We also show you what came out of the system, which for new Tecumseh builds is usually convincing on its own.

What tells me the dryer vent is the problem?

Longer cycles, heat in the laundry room, and the machine shutting off mid-load are the classics. We clear the full route and measure the flow, and if the appliance is genuinely the issue we say so instead of selling you a second visit.

Walk me through the visit.

Vacuum on, registers off and washed, every supply and return brushed while the system is under suction, blower compartment cleaned, and photos at the start and end. Figure three to four hours for most homes here, a bit more for the big two-storey plans.

Can you clear odours, not just dust?

Cleaning removes the residue that carries smells, and a sanitizer pass applied to the clean duct walls addresses what remains. That order, cleaning then treatment, is the only version that works, and it is the only one we sell.

Why is our new house dustier than our old one?

Often leftover construction dust still cycling through the system, sometimes leaking returns pulling attic or wall-cavity air in. Both are diagnosable, and we test before recommending anything.

Do you serve the communities around Tecumseh?

Yes, Windsor and Lakeshore including Belle River, Stoney Point, and Puce, with the rest of Essex County covered on request. We are minutes from all of it.
